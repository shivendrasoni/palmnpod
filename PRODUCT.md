## Users

**Primary: the caring chooser.** A health-conscious adult, often a mother, who makes food decisions for herself and others. Reads ingredients, thinks about refined sugar, wants something her family can enjoy, and still expects chocolate to feel like a treat. Does not want child-coded candy, joyless diet food, gym-coded protein snacks, vague wellness claims, or parenting guilt.

**Secondary: the considered indulger.** An adult buying for themselves: afternoon break, after dinner, on the go. Wants pleasure that fits their broader eating habits.

**Future: children.** May enjoy the product, but the masterbrand is not a children's brand. Any kids' range would be a separate sub-brand.

Source: `Brand identity.md` §6–7.

## Product Purpose

Palm and Pod makes deeply enjoyable chocolate food with considered ingredients, so care and pleasure can belong in the same moment. Purpose: make everyday pleasure feel caring rather than compromising.

The first product is the **Palm and Pod fudge brownie**. It has no product name for now (the "Spring" name in `Brand identity.md` is dropped by the owner, 2026-09-28; a name may be added later). The site is **pre-launch**: its job is to make people want the brownie and message to order. Nothing is sold on the site itself.

Success for the landing page: WhatsApp order messages from the primary audience, and visitors leaving with a clear sense of what the brownie is and what goes in it.

## Positioning

A healthy chocolate brand that does not look or talk like health food. Entry point is chocolate desire; ingredient quality is the reason to trust the choice. It sits where maternal warmth without childishness, health consideration without clinical language, indulgence without guilt messaging, premium without cold luxury, and ingredient honesty without rustic craft clichés meet.

Neighbors it must not resemble: child-coded candy, austere diet snacks, gym-first protein products.

Source: `Brand identity.md` §5.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Palm and Pod fudge brownie, verified for publishing (confirmed by the owner, 2026-09-28):**

- Descriptor: fudge brownie
- Made with dates, seeds (flax, sunflower, chia, pumpkin), butter, pure cocoa, whey protein, walnuts, cashews, and almonds (confirmed by the owner, 2026-09-28; `Brand identity.md` and `Design.md` still list only the first five)
- Contains protein from its recipe
- No refined sugar

**Allergens:** contains tree nuts (walnut, cashew, almond) and milk (butter, whey). Must be stated plainly and be easy to find on every product surface. The formal allergen declaration (including any may-contain statements) still needs to come from the production label.

**Never infer or state:** high protein, low calorie, low carb, diabetic-friendly, weight-loss support, gluten-free, allergen-free, organic, vegan, preservative-free, sugar-free, low sugar, healthy for children, or any clinical or performance benefit.

**Naming:** Masterbrand "Palm and Pod" (never P&P, Palm, or Pod). Product is called "Palm and Pod fudge brownie"; do not use "Spring" on any surface. Descriptors stay literal. Use "and", not "&", in prose.

## Brand Commitments

- Core idea: **nurturing indulgence**. Strategic centre: "Real chocolate joy, made with care." (not necessarily a tagline).
- Voice: warm not sugary, sensory not excessive, clear not clinical, confident not defensive, positive not guilt-based. At most one exclamation mark per piece, preferably none. Sentence case. Headlines about 3–10 words.
- Banned frames: guilt-free, cheat day, sinful, naughty, earned it, skinny, detox, superfood, fuel, gains, junk-free, and the rest of the list in `Brand identity.md` §17.
- Primary CTA: "Order on WhatsApp". Secondary: See the ingredients. Other CTAs follow the same voice.
- Ethics: never shame conventional chocolate, never imply a parent is better or a child more loved because of a purchase, no fear-based sugar or weight talk, body-neutral language, care shown as not exclusively female labour.

Full voice rules: `Brand identity.md` §14–24.

## Product Principles

1. **Desire first, proof second.** The brownie must look and sound delicious before any ingredient or nutrition fact appears.
2. **Every claim is a verified fact.** Only the confirmed brownie facts get published; everything else waits for evidence.
3. **Care means clarity.** Ingredients and dietary information are easy to find and plainly worded, never hidden or lectured.
4. **No guilt, no fear, no morality.** Pleasure is never framed as cheating, and caregivers are never pressured.
5. **Quiet confidence.** Warm, grounded, restrained. Fewer elements and fewer words, stated plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible keyboard focus. Minimum 16 px body text on mobile. Meaningful alt text for food and lifestyle images. No essential copy only inside images. Never signal meaning by colour alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Represent families without stereotyping gender, body type, age, or ability. Details in `Design.md` §4.4 and §17.
