# Palm and Pod — Landing page image brief

For: Spring pre-launch landing page (India).
Drop finished files in `brand/images/` using the exact filenames below. I convert, resize, and crop them in code.

## Before you generate

1. **Which seeds are in Spring?** Say which ones (pumpkin, sunflower, flax, sesame…). The generator must show only the real seeds. Wrong seeds break the brand's "never show an ingredient that isn't in the product" rule.
2. **Attach `brand/palm-and-pod-moodboard.png`** as a style reference if your tool accepts images.
3. **Use one brownie look throughout:** dense, very dark, fudgy, with visible seeds and chopped walnut, cashew and almond pieces in the crumb, and a soft crackled top. Cut squares, not round. Same brownie in every shot.

## Rules for every image

- **No text, logos, packaging, wrappers, or labels.** The wordmark isn't final. I add all text in code.
- **Light:** warm morning window light **from the left**, soft shadows. Keep the same direction in every shot.
- **Surfaces:** oat-toned paper, walnut wood, natural linen, unglazed ceramic, parchment. No marble, acrylic, chrome, or burlap.
- **Colour:** deep cocoa browns, oat cream, a touch of sage green (Pistachio Leaf `#87937A`), and a small glint of gold at most. Nothing saturated.
- **Texture:** real and imperfect: crumbs, broken edges, cocoa sheen. Not glossy, not dry, not floating ingredients.
- **Leave empty space** where noted. Headlines sit there.
- **Export:** PNG or high-quality JPG at the listed size or larger.

### Base prompt (start every prompt with this)

> Premium editorial food photography for a healthy chocolate brand. Dense dark fudge brownie with visible seeds, chopped walnut, cashew and almond pieces, and date texture, warm directional morning window light from the left, oat-toned paper, walnut, linen, and unglazed ceramic. Natural imperfections, rich appetising chocolate detail, soft shadows, restrained earth palette of deep cocoa brown, toffee, oat cream, muted sage green, a tiny butter-gold accent. Warm, caring, quiet, contemporary.

### Negative prompt (add to every image)

> text, letters, logo, label, packaging, wrapper, peanuts, hazelnuts, pistachios, pecans, chocolate chips, tropical, palm trees, coconut, gym, protein tub, shaker bottle, neon, saturated colours, cartoon, marble, chrome, plastic, overhead flash, excessive gloss, floating ingredients, splash, dry crumb, burnt

## Must-have (12 files)

### 1. `hero-desktop` — 2880 × 1800 (16:10)
**Role:** first screen on laptop. Says "you want this."
**Scene:** one brownie square broken in half by hand, just released. The fudgy centre faces the camera, with nut pieces showing in the break, and crumbs, a few seeds and a walnut piece on oat paper over walnut wood. Close three-quarter angle, shallow depth of field.
**Composition:** brownie in the **right 45%** of the frame. **Left 50% calm and plain** (soft oat paper, gentle shadow) for the headline.
**Add to prompt:** `macro three-quarter view, broken brownie revealing soft fudgy centre, crumbs on oat paper, subject on right, generous empty space on left`

### 2. `hero-mobile` — 1600 × 2000 (4:5)
**Role:** first screen on phone.
**Scene:** same as hero-desktop, recomposed vertically.
**Composition:** brownie in the **bottom 55%**. **Top 40% calm** for the headline.
**Add to prompt:** `vertical composition, broken brownie in lower half, calm oat paper space in upper half`

### 3. `texture-macro` — 2400 × 1600 (3:2)
**Role:** "A brownie that feels like a brownie." Appetite proof.
**Scene:** extreme macro of a clean cut edge showing the dense fudgy interior, cut cross-sections of walnut, cashew and almond, seed crunch, and a slight cocoa sheen on top. Fills the frame edge to edge. No empty space needed.
**Add to prompt:** `extreme macro of brownie cut face, dense moist fudgy crumb, walnut cashew and almond pieces and seeds embedded, fills the frame`

### 4–11. Ingredient set (8 cards) — 1200 × 1500 each (4:5)
**Role:** "What goes in matters." One ingredient per card.
**Consistency is the point:** same surface (oat paper on walnut), same light, same camera height (about 30° above), same ingredient size in frame, ingredient centred with calm space around it. They must look like one series.

| File | Scene |
|---|---|
| `ingredient-dates` | 4–5 soft, glossy dates in a small unglazed ceramic dish, one torn open showing sticky flesh. Not plastic-looking. |
| `ingredient-seeds` | A small, believable handful of **[your actual seeds]** spilled loosely on paper. Not a mountain. |
| `ingredient-butter` | A block of butter on parchment, one soft curl cut away with a butter knife. Warm and culinary, not excessive. |
| `ingredient-cocoa` | Pure cocoa powder in a small ceramic bowl, a little dusted onto the paper. Keep the fine powder texture visible. |
| `ingredient-walnut` | A few walnut halves, one cracked into pieces, on paper. Skinned or unskinned, matching what goes in the recipe. |
| `ingredient-cashew` | A small loose pile of whole cashews, one or two split in half. Pale and creamy, not roasted dark unless the recipe uses roasted. |
| `ingredient-almond` | A small loose pile of whole almonds, skin on unless the recipe uses blanched, one cut to show the inside. |
| `ingredient-whey` | Plain off-white powder in a small ceramic bowl with a wooden spoon. Should look like a baking ingredient. **No tub, scoop, shaker, or anything gym-like.** |

**Add to prompt (each):** `single ingredient still life, centred, small ceramic dish, oat paper on walnut, 30 degree angle, consistent series`

### 12. `og-share` — 1200 × 630
**Role:** preview image when the link is shared on WhatsApp, Instagram, and similar apps. People will see this before they see the site.
**Scene:** two or three brownie squares, one broken, on oat paper. Subject **centred**, left and right thirds calm. I may overlay the name in code.
**Add to prompt:** `wide composition, brownie squares centred, calm space on both sides`

## Nice-to-have (2 files)

### 13. `sharing-hands` — 2400 × 1600 (3:2)
**Role:** care through gesture. "For the people you care about. Including you."
**Scene:** one pair of hands passing a broken brownie piece to another hand across a linen-covered table. **Faces cropped out.** Indian skin tones, believable texture, natural nails, no jewellery overload. **Don't make it only a mother's hand.** Could be two adults, a grandparent and a child, or friends.
**Add to prompt:** `hands sharing a broken brownie across a linen table, faces out of frame, observational crop, natural skin texture`

### 14. `closing-still` — 2880 × 1400 (wide banner)
**Role:** background for the final "Message us on WhatsApp" section.
**Scene:** a small stack of brownie squares on parchment with a ceramic cup nearby, late warm light. Overall **darker** (cocoa tones dominate) so cream text reads on top.
**Composition:** stack on the **right third**. Left two thirds dark and quiet.
**Add to prompt:** `low-key darker image, stack of brownies on right, deep cocoa shadows, quiet dark space on left`
